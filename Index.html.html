What changed — and why most businesses haven't noticed

Search used to show ten businesses. AI answers name one or two. There's no page one anymore — there's the answer, and there's everyone else.

AI picks its recommendation from a handful of signals — most of them living in your Google Business Profile. Here's exactly what it checks, and what it costs you when each one slips:

SignalWhy it wins you customersWhat neglecting it costs you
Reviews replied to within 24–48h AI reads your replies as proof you're active and accountable — and customers judge you by how you handle the bad ones, not the good ones. Unanswered reviews read like an abandoned shop. AI downgrades you; customers pick whoever bothered to respond.
Fresh photos, added regularly Recent photos are a "we're alive and busy" signal — and customers decide in about 3 seconds of scrolling them. Photos from 2021 make people wonder if you still trade. Some will literally drive to a competitor instead of risking it.
Posts & updates from the owner Every post is fresh, keyword-rich content AI can read — offers, jobs done, seasonal services. A silent profile tells AI a competitor's active one is the safer recommendation. Silence is a ranking decision.
Correct categories & services This is how AI matches you to the question. Right categories = you're in the running for your best-paying jobs. One wrong or missing category and you're invisible for entire types of work — you'll never know those calls existed.
Accurate hours "Open now" is in a huge share of urgent, high-value searches — emergencies, same-day jobs. Wrong hours = a customer at a locked door, a 1-star review, and AI quietly dropping you from "open now" answers.
Consistent info across the web AI cross-checks your name, number and address across directories. Matching info = a business it can trust. An old address or a dead phone number on some directory you forgot about makes AI unsure — and unsure means skipped.

The profile AI skips

How is this different from SEO?

Traditional SEO is about ranking your website in a list of links. This is about being the business an AI actually names when someone asks it a question. The work is different too — it lives mostly in your Google Business Profile, your reviews, and how consistently your business appears across the web, not in blog posts and backlinks.

How quickly will I see more customers?

Google profile improvements often show within days. AI answers typically move within 2–6 weeks. Anyone promising instant results in this space is having you on. What we'd say instead: the effect compounds — every month you're active while your competitors aren't makes you harder to knock off.

Do you need my passwords or logins?

Never. You add us as a manager on your Google profile (two clicks, Google's own feature) and can remove us anytime. Everything — the profile, the reviews, the reputation — belongs to you.
